Our Products

A glance of our products

Mrs. Balls Chutney is a sweet, fruity, tangy,
and mildly spiced South African condiment.
Flavour: Sweet spicy, Sweety mild.
Core ingredients: Made from blended of
dried fruits such as peaches, apricots.
         
Chakalaka is a South African mixture of different
vegetables including Beans and Sweetcorn.
Flavours: Spicy, Savory, and Tangy.
Core ingredients: Onions, Tomatoes, Carrots,
Bell Peppers, Chili, and Baked Beans.
         
Biltong is a South African Air dried, cured meat
made from thick cuts of beef meat.
Flavour: Savory, Salty, and Tangy.
Core ingredients: Vinegar, Salt, Spices.
         
Ouma Rusks are South African biscuits traditionally
made for dipping into morning coffee or tea.
Flavours: ButterMilk, Condensed Milk.
         
Freshpak Rooibos Tea is a South African number one
Rooibos tea brand, made from 100%
pure Aspalathus Linearis leaves.
Flavours: Mixed Fruits Rooibis Tea,
Original Rooibos Tea.
         
Nik Naks are South African iconic, crunchy extruded
maize (corn) puffs.
Flavours: Original Cheese, Fruit Chutney, Spicy Beef/
Beef BBQ, Spicy Chicken, Flamin Hot Chilli.
