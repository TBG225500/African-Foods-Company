Our Story

AFC is the place that gives everyone an opportunity to get a taste of South African snacks.

From our head office in Cape Town, we work with our sites in Durban, Gqeberha and Polokwane to bring authentic flavours to communities around the world.

Every product we ship carries a little piece of home.

Mission

To share the authentic taste of South Africa with the world through quality food products that connect people to their heritage and culture.

Vision

To become a leading global supplier of South African food products,bringing the flavours of home to communities around the world.

Our Promise

To deliver authentic, high-quality South African food products that bring people closer to home, wherever they are.
