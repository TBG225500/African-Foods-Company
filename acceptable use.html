Acceptable use

Rules for using computers, email and networks.

Purpose

The purpose of the acceptance use policy is to define how users can utilize the organisations IT resources (West, 2024). This policy helps prevent misuse of African Foods Company’s resources and information by informing employees about how they should use the resources.

Guidelines

  • Acceptable Use: Company IT resources must be used for authorised business activities. Limited personal use must not interfere with the work or company operations.
  • Prohibited Use: Users must not access, distribute or store illegal, offensive or inappropriate content, attempt unauthorised access, introduce malware or use company systems for fraudulent or malicious activities.
  • Internet and email usage: Employees must use the internet and email services appropriately and must not access illegal, inappropriate or harmful content.
  • Security: Users must protect their passwords and accounts, use strong passwords, lock devices when not in use and report suspected security incidents immediately. Employees should also avoid clicking on suspicious links.
  • Data protection: The company’s sensitive information must be kept confidential and only accessed, copied or shared when authorised.
  • Software Installation: Employees are prohibited from installing unauthorised and unlicensed software on the company’s devices. Only authorised users may update the company’s website and systems.
  • Incident reporting: Employees must report suspected misuse, malware, data breaches, and suspected phishing or unauthorised access to the seniors.
  • Monitoring: African Foods Company may monitor the use of its IT resources to maintain security, investigate misuse and ensure compliance with this policy.
  • Enforcement: Failure to comply with this policy may result in the restriction of system access and appropriate disciplinary or legal action.
  • Policy review: This policy should be reviewed constantly and updated when the company’s systems, security requirements and business operations change.
