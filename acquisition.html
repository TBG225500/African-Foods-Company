Acquisition

How to request and approve IT hardware, software and services.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that network infrastructure equipment, software, and hardware are purchased cost-effectively.

Request and approval steps

  1. Request Initiation:Requests are submitted by employees or IT for new equipment or replacements.
  2. Request review: The IT Manager must identify and submit requests for network infrastructure resources
    required for the project. Each request must state what is needed, why it is needed, and what it will be used for (Sukhareva, 2026).
  3. Technical Assessment: The IT Manager must assess the requested equipment or software to ensure that it will work/collaborate with the company’s existing network, meet security requirements and the company’s needs (Sukhareva, 2026).
  4. Quotations: Quotations must be obtained from suitable suppliers. Quotations should be compared based on cost, quality, technical specifications, supplier reliability, warranty, and support services (FasterCapital, 2026).
  5. Approvals: All acquisitions must be authorised by IT manager or authorised user before an order is placed. Over budget purchases may require authorisation from senior management (Sukhareva, 2026).
  6. Invoice & Finance Verification: Finance verifies supplier, budget availability and approves supplier payment.
  7. Procurement: After approval, the authorised user must purchase the equipment, software, or services from an approved supplier and follow the company’s purchasing procedures (Sukhareva, 2026).
  8. Delivery & Verification: IT department checks delivery against purchase order.
  9. Setup & Configuration: “IT department installs, configures, migrates data and trains or onboards the user” (The Da Vinci Institute, 2026).
  10. Asset Tagging: IT department labels equipment with a unique asset number and updates Finance and related asset tracking systems (The Da Vinci Institute, 2026).
  11. Asset Registration: All purchased network equipment and other IT assets must be recorded in the company’s asset register. Records should include the asset description, serial number, location, supplier, purchase date, and the department or user responsible for it (Sukhareva, 2026)./li>
  12. Handover & Acknowledgement: Employees or the relevant stakeholder signs handover form; IT countersigns; copies sent to Finance and HR are stored and captured with the related asset tracking system.
  13. Finance Confirmation: Finance verifies receipt with the user.
  14. Asset Register Update: Asset details are recorded in the central register.
  15. Resignation, retirement, dismissal: Assets to be returned on their last day, as per the instruction and confirmation by the People Management department.
  16. Record-Keeping: All acquisition documentation, including requests, technical assessments, quotations, approvals, purchase orders, invoices, warranties, licences, and asset records, must be securely maintained for accountability, auditing, and future reference (FasterCapital, 2026)
