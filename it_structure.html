IT structure

Purpose

The purpose of this report is to outline the reasons for and importance of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and why they should be implemented in businesses. SOPs have a huge impact on companies and their operations because they ensure compliance with rules and regulations, guide their employees in performing their daily tasks correctly, and ensures that they can tackle any obstacle that comes their ways.

IT responsibilities

  • IT Manager: leads the IT team, approves IT purchases and signs off policies.
  • Systems Administrator: looks after servers, user accounts, software and backups.
  • Network Administrator: looks after the network, internet links, Wi-Fi and firewalls.

Escalation path

  1. Staff member logs the issue with the service desk.
  2. Systems or Network Administrator resolves it.
  3. If unresolved, it goes to the IT Manager.
  4. The IT Manager escalates serious issues to company management.
