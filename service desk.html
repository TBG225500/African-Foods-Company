Service desk

How to report and track IT issues.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to provide a clear process for reporting and resolving IT and network problems that African Foods Company may encounter.

  • Reporting IT Problems:Employees must report IT or network problems to the Help Desk as soon as possible. The report should include the user’s name, location, device, and a short description of the problem (Valle, 2026).
  • Recording Requests:The Help Desk must record each reported problem and provide a reference number where necessary. This helps the IT team track the progress of each request and ensure that it is resolved before tackling other issues.
  • Issue Priority:Problems must be given a priority based on how seriously they affect African Foods Company. Problems affecting the network or the business operations should receive urgent attention (Valle, 2026).
  • Troubleshooting:The IT team must investigate the reported problem and perform basic troubleshooting to identify the cause and resolve it.
  • Escalation management:If the Help Desk cannot solve the problem, it must be escalated to the IT Manager, Network Administrator or the company’s ISP depending on the complexity of the problem (Valle, 2026)
  • Resolution:The IT team must fix the problem and confirm with the affected employee that the service is working correctly before closing the request.
  • Access management:The IT team must create, change, and remove user access to company systems based on their influence and impact. Access must only be given to authorised users and removed when it is no longer needed, or an employee is no longer part of the company (Valle, 2026).
  • Password and account recovery:The IT Help Desk must assist users who have forgotten their passwords or are unable to access their accounts. User identity must be verified before access is restored or passwords are reset (Valle, 2026).
  • User onboarding and offboarding:The IT team must provide new employees with the required accounts, devices, and system access. When an employee leaves the company, their accounts and access must be removed, and company equipment must be returned as they are under the company’s ownership (Valle, 2026).
  • Record-Keeping:All reported problems, actions taken, and solutions must be recorded and kept for future reference and to help identify repeated network problems and could possibly be used as a reference to resolve similar issues that the company might encounter (Valle, 2026).

Priority levels

PriorityExampleTarget response
CriticalNetwork or server down for a siteWithin 1 hour
HighOne department cannot workWithin 4 hours
MediumOne user cannot workWithin 1 working day
LowRequest or minor faultWithin 3 working days

Tracking and closing

  • The assigned technician updates the ticket as work progresses.
  • The user confirms the issue is fixed.
  • The ticket is closed and the fix recorded.
